# VocabCraft

## AI-powered Personal Knowledge Memory System


Turn your daily questions into long-term knowledge.


## Why VocabCraft?

Traditional learning starts from textbooks.

VocabCraft starts from real-life questions.


Every day we meet:

- unfamiliar English words
- technical documents
- professional concepts
- communication barriers


Instead of forgetting these questions,

VocabCraft transforms them into personal knowledge assets.


Question

↓

Capture

↓

AI Understanding

↓

Practice

↓

Memory

# Origin Story


I have 20 years of SAP FI/CO implementation experience.

During enterprise projects, I discovered:

Many professionals are not limited by technical skills.

They are limited by language communication.


VocabCraft was created to solve my own problem first.
